Who We Are

A Community-Centered
Healthcare Facility

Located in the Soweto area of Embakasi East Sub-County (Lower Savannah), next to Soweto Catholic Parish in Nairobi County, Blessed to Bless Healthcare is a modern, patient-centered health facility committed to delivering comprehensive and affordable healthcare services to communities in Nairobi and its environs.

Established to respond to urgent healthcare needs within Nairobi's underserved communities, we have grown from a modest clinic into a Level 3A healthcare facility. We embrace digital health systems to enhance efficiency and work closely with government programs to provide immunization services and social health insurance schemes.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Healthcare Services

A full spectrum of healthcare offerings designed with you in mind.

Maternity Services

Antenatal & Postnatal care, child welfare clinics with expert midwifery support.

Laboratory Services

Comprehensive diagnostic tests including blood work, cultures, hormonal analysis, and more.

Pharmacy Services

On-site pharmacy with a wide range of medications, ensuring continuity of care.

Dental Care

Professional dental services ranging from routine check-ups to specialized dental procedures.

GBV Support Services

Specialized, confidential, and compassionate support for survivors of gender-based violence.

Minor Surgical Procedures

Two fully equipped operating theatres offering safe, quality surgical procedures.

HIV Counseling & Testing

Confidential VCT services with counseling, support, and linkage to care.

Family Planning

Wide range of family planning services tailored to individual and couple needs.

Youth-Friendly Services

Safe, inclusive, and non-judgmental healthcare services designed for the youth.

SHA Services

Social Health Authority services to ensure accessible and insured healthcare for all.

Outpatient & Inpatient

Modern outpatient consultation and 40-bed inpatient services for comprehensive care.

Caregiver Training

Structured training programs to empower caregivers in delivering quality home-based care.
